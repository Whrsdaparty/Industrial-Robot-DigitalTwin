# Commissioning Checklist

Purpose: step-by-step startup procedure understandable by technicians.

## Pre-Power
- Verify grounding
- Verify protection devices
- Verify wiring against schematics
- Verify E-stop continuity

## Initial Power
- Apply control power
- Verify PLC online
- Verify safety relay status

## Motion
- Verify servo directions
- Verify homing
- Verify travel limits

## Digital Twin
- Verify ROS2 communication
- Verify joint positions match twin
- Verify alarms propagate
